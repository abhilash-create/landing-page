    Frequently asked

    Everything you need to know before you switch.

    How do I keep my current number?

    During checkout, select "Transfer my existing number" and provide your carrier's account details. Your number transfers within 1–3 business days with zero service interruption.

    Can I bring my own device?

    Yes! Most unlocked smartphones from the past 5 years are compatible. Use the IMEI checker above to verify in seconds. If carrier-locked, ask your provider to unlock it first.

    Is there really no contract?

    None whatsoever. Nu Mobile is month-to-month. Cancel any time directly from your account — no early termination fees, no questions asked.

    What network does Nu Mobile use?

    Nu Mobile runs on America's largest and most reliable 5G network — same towers as the big carriers, covering 99% of the US population, at a fraction of the price.

    How quickly can I get started?

    Setup takes just minutes online. SIM arrives in 3–5 business days; activate instantly from the app. eSIM available on compatible devices for same-day activation.

    How do I know if my phone is unlocked?

    Insert a SIM from a different carrier. If calls and data work, you're unlocked. You can also check Settings › Carrier or call your current provider to confirm.
